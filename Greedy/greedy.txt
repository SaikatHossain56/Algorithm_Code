Main Points:
a. Optimizations, min, max, no. of ways
b. sort/ priority queue to easily get local optimal.

Time complexities: O(n) or O(n log n)
Can generate near optimal solutions

Limitations:
Suboptimal solutions: local optimal may not ensure global optimal
No backtracking: Never reconsider choices, this may miss out better solutions.

Greedy Choice Property:
locally optimal ---> global optimal
If we choose the best solution at any moment it will help generating the overall best solution.

Optimal Substructure:
The optimal solution to a problem contains the optimal solutions to its subproblems
Means Solving each subproblem optimally will leads to the overall optimal solution.

1. Dijkstra's Algorithm:
At each step, it chooses the vertex with shortest distance.
